# Chibi miniature-cat diorama — image-gen prompt

Saved for later. NOT wired into the current `make_scene` pipeline — this is a
reference prompt for turning a real-cat photo into a premium collectible-figurine
chibi diorama (identity-preserving, ~70% original cat / 30% stylization), kept on
the original cushion and storytelling moment. Feed it to an image-gen/edit model
with the source photo (and a chibi reference sample) when we want a stylized variant.

---

Add a premium chibi-style miniature cat diorama onto the cushion while preserving the original composition, atmosphere, and storytelling moment of the scene.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
CRITICAL OBJECTIVE
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

The goal is NOT to create a cute cat.

The goal is to create the cutest possible collectible miniature version of THIS EXACT CAT.

The owner of the cat should immediately recognize their cat before noticing the stylization.

Target balance:

* 70% original cat identity
* 30% premium chibi stylization

Recognition is more important than stylization.

The result should feel like:

"A luxury handcrafted collectible figurine of the exact cat shown in the photo."

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
STEP 1: ANALYZE THE CAT
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

Before generating, carefully identify and preserve:

* Cat breed and breed-specific characteristics
* Fur color
* Fur pattern
* Face structure
* Eye shape
* Eye color
* Ear shape
* Nose shape
* Muzzle shape
* Forehead shape
* Facial expression
* Body proportions
* Distinctive visual traits
* Accessories and decorations
* Characteristic posture

Do not generate a generic cute cat.

Do not redesign the cat.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
CAT IDENTITY PRESERVATION
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

Preserve:

* Original facial structure
* Original eye shape
* Original muzzle shape
* Original forehead shape
* Original expression
* Original breed characteristics
* Original visual identity

The cat must still feel like the same individual cat.

If the owner would not immediately recognize their cat, the result is incorrect.

Do not simplify the cat into a generic cartoon animal.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
STORY & MOMENT PRESERVATION
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

The story of the photo is part of the cat's identity.

Before generating, identify the most memorable visual moment in the image.

Preserve the key action, interaction, emotion, pose, or story occurring in the original photo.

Examples:

* Smelling a flower
* Hugging a plush toy
* Looking out a window
* Sleeping on a cushion
* Resting with a specific object
* Interacting with a decoration
* Wearing a unique accessory
* Hiding inside a blanket
* Resting in a characteristic pose

The miniature diorama should recreate the same moment.

Do NOT replace the original behavior with a generic sitting pose.

The most memorable moment of the photo must remain visible.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
CUTENESS DIRECTION
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

Cuteness should come from:

* Emotional expression
* Miniature proportions
* Premium craftsmanship
* Collectible figurine aesthetics
* Warm atmosphere
* Charming pose
* Soft presentation
* Cozy storytelling

NOT from changing the cat's identity.

The cat should remain recognizable first and cute second.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
ACCESSORIES & ENVIRONMENTAL DETAILS
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

First priority:

Use any accessories that actually appear on the cat.

Examples:

* Hats
* Clothing
* Costumes
* Collars
* Decorative accessories
* Unique ornaments

Faithfully reinterpret them in miniature collectible form.

Second priority:

If the original image contains beautiful surrounding elements such as:

* Plush toys
* Decorative ornaments
* Flowers
* Cushions
* Miniature objects
* Fabrics
* Cozy room decorations
* Cute props
* Seasonal decorations

These existing elements may be incorporated into the miniature diorama.

Important:

Only use elements that already exist in the original image.

Do NOT invent new accessories.

Do NOT add:

* Crowns
* Ribbons
* Jewelry
* Wings
* Fantasy items
* Random flowers that are not present
* Additional costumes
* Unrelated decorations
* New props that do not exist in the scene

Every decorative element must originate from the original photo.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
STYLE
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

* Premium miniature diorama
* Luxury collectible figurine
* High-end resin toy quality
* Handcrafted designer toy aesthetic
* Ultra cute but elegant
* Warm emotional storytelling
* Cozy atmosphere
* Appealing to women in their late 20s to early 40s

NOT photorealistic.

NOT realistic animal rendering.

NOT hyper-realistic fur.

NOT real-cat proportions.

The result should clearly look like a collectible figure rather than a real cat.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
FACE & VIEWING DIRECTION
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

Front-facing composition preferred.

The cat should generally face toward the viewer.

However:

If the original storytelling moment requires a different viewing direction (for example smelling a flower, looking at an object, interacting with a toy, or looking through a window), preserve that interaction naturally.

Storytelling is more important than forcing eye contact.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
PLACEMENT & SCALE
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

Position the cat completely on the cushion.

Center the cat naturally within the cushion.

Occupy approximately 90% of the cushion surface.

No body parts may extend beyond the cushion boundary.

The composition should feel balanced and intentional.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
PAWS
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

Hide all paw pads.

No visible paw beans.

Tuck paws naturally underneath the body.

Avoid exposing the underside of the feet.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
RESTRICTIONS
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

No text.

No speech bubbles.

No emojis.

No floating icons.

No extra characters.

No exaggerated visual effects.

No unrelated objects.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
RENDERING
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

* Premium 3D miniature diorama
* High-end collectible figure quality
* Rich handcrafted details
* Soft cinematic lighting
* Warm cozy color palette
* Display-worthy craftsmanship
* Emotional and charming presentation

Reference the sample image for:

* Chibi proportions
* Miniature craftsmanship quality
* Collectible figure aesthetics
* Emotional warmth
* Diorama presentation

Preserve the identity, story, accessories, and memorable moment of the original cat above everything else.
